What Does the 3D MIT Show?
The 3D tube shows your casing string from outside, as if you cut open the wellbore and look at it. Each horizontal ring is one depth station. Each ring has 24 fingers measuring the radius from the tool centre to the casing wall around the full 360° circumference.

Green rings = casing is round and near nominal ID. Amber/orange rings = moderate reduction (scale, corrosion). Red rings = severe restriction (scale, damage, deformation). Cyan rings = enlarged (corrosion pitting, casing collar).

The tube runs top to bottom = shallow to deep. Depth labels are shown on the left axis. Use the Depth Slider below the 3D to sweep a cross-section through the tube at any depth.
